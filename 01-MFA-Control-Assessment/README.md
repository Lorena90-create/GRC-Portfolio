# MFA Security Control Assessment

**Framework:** NIST SP 800-53 — IA-2
**Project Type:** Educational simulation
**Status:** In progress

## Objective
Evaluate whether multi-factor authentication (MFA) is implemented effectively and identify potential security risks.

## Scenario
In a fictional organization, two privileged user accounts were identified without MFA enabled, despite a policy requiring MFA for all employee accounts.

## Control Finding
The MFA control is not fully implemented because two privileged accounts do not comply with the organization's authentication policy.

## Preliminary Risk Assessment
- Likelihood: 4/5
- Impact: 4/5
- Risk Score: 16/25
- Risk Rating: High

The ratings are preliminary and will be supported by a documented risk matrix and justification.

## Recommended Remediation
Enable MFA for the affected privileged accounts, investigate the cause of the exception, review account provisioning procedures, and establish periodic MFA compliance checks.

## Next Steps
- Document simulated assessment evidence
- Justify likelihood and impact ratings
- Identify remediation owners and target dates
- Define retesting procedures

## Disclaimer
This is a fictional educational assessment. No confidential employer or client information is included.
